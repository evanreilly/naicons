03 / WHY THIS EXISTS

More than the
usual skylines.

To my knowledge, there isn’t an icon pack for North American cities that goes much beyond the megacities. So, for TrainGame, I made one.

This collection covers every city above 100,000 people in the game’s US, Canada, and Mexico inventory, plus five smaller cities to give states without a 100k city a place on the map. Burlington, Vermont, is one of them.

The small print, and the small cities +

The five exceptions are Burlington, Vermont; Charleston, West Virginia; Cheyenne, Wyoming; Portland, Maine; and Wilmington, Delaware.

“North America” here means the United States, Canada, and Mexico—not a claim to cover every country on the continent. Populations come from the source inventory: US 2024 estimates, Canadian 2025 estimates, and Mexico’s 2020 census. City boundaries and definitions differ by country; these are not metro-area rankings.

The artwork was generated with Nano Banana Pro and prepared as transparent 1024 × 1024 PNGs. The icons carry a CC0 dedication for any rights I may hold; this doesn’t assert exclusive copyright in AI-generated artwork. The site code is MIT-licensed. Full provenance and population sources are in the repository.
