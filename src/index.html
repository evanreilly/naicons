About these icons

To my knowledge, there isn’t an icon pack covering North American cities beyond the megacities. This is the TrainGame collection: 544 cities across the US, Canada, and Mexico, covering the game’s cities above 100k plus five smaller exceptions for states without a 100k city: Burlington VT, Charleston WV, Cheyenne WY, Portland ME, and Wilmington DE.

Population years: US 2024, Canada 2025, Mexico 2020. Geographic definitions vary; these are not metro populations. Generated with Nano Banana Pro. CC0 for any rights I may hold; code MIT. Sources and originals ↗
